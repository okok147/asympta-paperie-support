Privacy

Asympta Paperie Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

Asympta Paperie is designed to keep journal content under the user's control. The current release does not use advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral tracking. Paperie does not sell personal information.

Notebook data

Notebook content can include handwriting, text, notebook and page names, cover and paper choices, stationery, drawings, and images you choose to add. This content is stored on your device and, when iCloud synchronization is enabled and available, may also be stored in your iCloud environment so supported content can synchronize across your devices.

iCloud

Paperie may use Apple iCloud or CloudKit services for synchronization and recovery. Apple processes the related cloud data under Apple's terms and privacy policies. Paperie does not use notebook content for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Photos

When you choose to import a photo or image for a notebook or stationery feature, Paperie processes the item for the feature you requested. A copy may be stored with Paperie data and may synchronize through iCloud when synchronization is enabled. Paperie does not use imported images for advertising.

Purchases

The optional Lifetime unlock is processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Paperie uses purchase entitlement information to determine whether Lifetime features should be unlocked. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.

Support email

If you contact support, your email address, message, and anything you voluntarily attach are processed by the email services involved and are used to respond to the support request. Do not send private notebook content unless it is needed to diagnose the issue.

Retention and deletion

  • Local Paperie data remains until you delete the relevant content or remove the app, subject to device backup behavior.
  • If iCloud synchronization is enabled, synchronized copies may remain in iCloud until deleted through the app or the applicable Apple data-management mechanisms.
  • Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, reasonable follow-up, security, or legal obligations.

Your choices

You can choose whether to import photos and whether to use available cloud features. You can delete notebooks from Paperie. You can manage iCloud and app permissions in Apple system settings. Restorable Lifetime purchases can be refreshed using Restore Purchases in the app.

Changes to this policy

If Paperie's data practices change, this policy will be updated so that it continues to describe the shipping app accurately.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information controlled by the developer, contact hello@everformlab.com.

Asympta Paperie

A quiet handwriting notebook for iPhone and iPad.

Writing and pages

Use Apple Pencil for direct handwriting on supported iPad models. You can also work with notebooks, pages, covers, paper styles, stationery, and imported images using the controls provided in the app.

Notebooks

The free experience includes one notebook. The Lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase that unlocks additional notebooks. Lifetime does not auto-renew.

Lifetime purchase

Lifetime is purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. The App Store shows the final local price before confirmation. Payment and purchase authorization are handled by Apple.

If you already bought Lifetime, use Restore Purchases in Paperie to refresh the entitlement for the Apple Account currently used by the App Store.

iCloud

When iCloud synchronization is available and enabled, Paperie can keep supported notebook data in the user's iCloud environment. If content does not appear on another device, first confirm that both devices use the intended Apple Account, iCloud is enabled, and the network connection is available.

Deleting or changing a notebook

Notebook management is available from the notebook library. Deleting a notebook removes that notebook from Paperie. If iCloud synchronization is enabled, the deletion may also synchronize to other devices.

Photos and stationery

Paperie only uses photos you choose for notebook or stationery features. Avoid importing sensitive material that you do not want stored with a notebook or synchronized through your enabled cloud services.

Need help?

Email hello@everformlab.com. Include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of the problem. Please do not include private notebook content unless it is necessary for support.
